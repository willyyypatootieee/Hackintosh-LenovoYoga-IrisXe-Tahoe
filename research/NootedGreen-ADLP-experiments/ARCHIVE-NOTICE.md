# Historical local ADL-P experiments

These are local NootedGreen source/scripts retained for research recovery,
including Sequoia experiments. They are **not the provenance of every deployed
Tahoe binary**, not a recommended installer and not completed HDMI support.
Old notes are historical and must not override the main Yoga status/recovery
documentation. MacKernelSDK, Lilu and Apple driver payloads are excluded.
